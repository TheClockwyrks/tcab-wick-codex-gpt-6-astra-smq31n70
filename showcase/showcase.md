# Keep the light

One lamplighter. A city full of wings. Ten minutes until dawn. Wick is a survival game about finding a path through a night that keeps closing in.

You only walk. Your tools slash, orbit, spark, and burn on their own rhythms. Turn toward a closing moth, slip between a pack of hounds, and double back for the gems your light leaves behind.

Every level offers a choice: carry a new tool, strengthen an old favorite, or take a trinket that changes your whole kit. Bring a mastered tool and its matching trinket to a chest, and watch it transform.

The first moth is only the beginning. Swarms cross the streets, great winged creatures guard chests, and at nine minutes the Dark comes for your lamp. Keep moving. Dawn is closer than it looks.
